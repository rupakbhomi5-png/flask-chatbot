Workmanship and Warranty Policy

All roof replacements include a 5-year labor warranty covering installation
defects, separate from the shingle manufacturer's own material warranty.
Architectural shingle lines we install typically carry a 30-year limited
manufacturer warranty against manufacturing defects; the exact term depends
on the shingle line the homeowner selects during the estimate. The labor
warranty does not cover damage from severe weather events, tree strikes, or
work performed by another contractor after our install. A written warranty
copy is provided at project completion, not just quoted verbally.

Emergency and Storm Response

For active leaks or storm damage, same-day tarping is offered when a crew is
available in the area, typically within 4 to 8 hours of the call during
business hours. Emergency tarp service carries a flat 150 dollar dispatch
fee, which is credited back toward the final repair invoice if the customer
proceeds with the full repair through us. After-hours emergency calls
outside 7am to 7pm carry an additional 75 dollar after-hours fee on top of
the standard dispatch fee. Tarping is a temporary measure only; a full
inspection and repair estimate follows within 2 business days.

Financing Options

Roof replacements can be financed over 12, 24, or 60 months through our
third-party financing partner, subject to credit approval. The 12-month
plan carries no interest if paid in full within the term. Estimates are
always free regardless of whether financing is used. A financing pre-
qualification does not affect credit score and takes about 2 minutes to
complete on the estimate call or in person.

Service Area and Response Time

We currently serve a roughly 40-mile radius around the metro area. Standard
estimate requests are scheduled within 3 business days of the initial
inquiry. Storm-damage estimate requests are prioritized and typically
scheduled within 24 to 48 hours when a named storm or hail event has hit the
area. Requests from outside the standard service radius are still logged
and offered a callback, but a site visit may take longer to schedule or may
require a small travel fee, confirmed before any visit is booked.

Roof Inspection Process

A standard inspection covers shingle condition, flashing around chimneys
and vents, gutter attachment points, and attic ventilation from the inside
when accessible. Inspections take 30 to 45 minutes on a typical single-story
home. Photos are taken throughout and included in the written estimate. A
free inspection does not obligate the homeowner to book work; there is no
high-pressure follow-up beyond one scheduled check-in call about a week
after the estimate is sent.

Insurance Claim Assistance

When roof damage is likely storm-related, we can meet the homeowner's
insurance adjuster on-site at no extra charge and provide our own written
damage assessment to support the claim. We do not file the claim on the
homeowner's behalf and do not guarantee any claim will be approved — that
decision is the insurance company's alone. We are not a public adjuster and
do not charge a percentage of the claim payout; our pricing is the same
whether a job is insurance-funded or paid directly.

Material Options

Standard three-tab shingles, architectural (dimensional) shingles, and
metal standing-seam roofing are all offered. Architectural shingles are the
most commonly chosen option and what our standard estimate defaults to
unless the homeowner asks about metal or a premium designer shingle line.
Color and style samples are shown during the in-person estimate; a full
manufacturer sample book is also available on request.

Satisfaction Policy

If a homeowner is not satisfied with completed work, we return at no charge
within 30 days of project completion to address workmanship concerns
covered under the labor warranty above. This does not cover changes of mind
about material or color selected before installation — those are confirmed
with the homeowner in writing before any material is ordered.
